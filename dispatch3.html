Cross-cutting themes

GGC as an anchoring force for change

The process itself is providing a stabilising backbone for the org. Multiple people independently noted this "nervous system regulation" effect. Beyond individual experiment outcomes, the process is generating value.

Financial sustainability remains an open thread

The day rate gap and absence of a dedicated finance-governance experiment will be built towards at the May review. Work has been ongoing to increase financial data awareness across Mission Pathways, Capacity, Finance and COF.

Strategy clarity is a persistent gap

We still can't collectively state "this is the Dm strategy." The 10x100 and sensemaking sessions are helping, but the gap between sensing and a stated direction remains. Another collective sensemaking is planned for the next 10x100.

(Re-)onboarding is emerging as a theme

Several experiments are creating new structures the wider team needs to understand. Ideas converging around using new hire moments and new membership formation of groups like Health Keepers and Dm Stewards.

GGC Experiment #1

Dm Stewards: Nominations Are In In Progress

Sophia launched the Dm Stewards nomination process on April 30, opening a question that's been sitting with the team: How do we hold Dm's mission and ambition in a more distributed and intentional way?

The numbers from nominations:

  • 39 people were nominated overall
  • 9 people self-nominated
  • 5 people received 7+ nominations
  • 6 people received 5–6 nominations

"What the process has surfaced very clearly is the depth of stewardship, care, leadership and responsibility-holding that already exists across Dm."

A small working group of Sophia, Jorn, and Annette is now exploring possible pathways forward, currently orienting toward a steward group of around 12 people for this first phase. They're balancing: frequency of peer nominations, diversity across the system, complementary experience, representation across Dm's work, and the need for the group to remain coherent and workable.

The 6-month experiment runs May–October, with a review at the October gathering.

Working group: Sophia, Jorn, Annette · On behalf of: People Team & Health Keepers

Future of Being Human: First DM Open Hours 🤖

On May 20, DM hosted its first Open Hours — a conversational (not webinar!) space exploring "What could investing in the human side of the machine age actually look like?" Speakers included Eunsoo, Adam, Indy, plus the teams behind Aidan Cinnamon Tea and the Lagori collective.

The conversation was, in Adam's words, "deep, generous, and timely." A new microsite is now live at dark-matter-labs.github.io/future_of_being_human, with thoughts welcome on the emerging provocation and portfolio of work.

Speakers: Eunsoo, Adam, Indy · Hosting: Amelia · Microsite: Human Machine Futures team

Permissioning the City: Korea Workshop + Beta Launch

The South Korea team hosted a workshop with RMIT and UTS architecture professors exploring how temporary/micro architecture could create new spatial opportunities in Incheon. RMIT sent flatpack units for local fabrication, and participants used Permissioning the City to explore activating space differently.

The beta launch followed mid-May, bringing together core features: space registry, risk routing, conditional rule adjustment, deliberation flows, social identity (vouching/endorsement), payments and smart contracts, multi-verification systems, and role-change nudges. These will be tested across 40+ local sites over the next 5 months.

A series of three blogs (in English and Korean) is being prepared, unpacking why permissioning, digital trust, and protocol-driven governance matter — framing Permissioning the City as a Civic Ledger.

Project lead: Eunji · Team: Calvin, Koo, Eden, Kai, Fang, Ahyoung, Simon, Joost, Jungwon, Doeun, Sungwon
