Welcome back

Hello team,

October was a month of things getting out of the door. Louise’s book is out with Routledge, Anette, Amelia, and Joshua published on why proven urban solutions do not scale, and Indy’s conversation on The Great Simplification went live. This issue spans Halloween, the Day of the Dead, and the run-up to Diwali on 8 November, so the web version has a few things hidden in it. Happy hunting, and Shubh Deepavali to everyone celebrating.

New work

HDCE and Homes England

On 15 October Max announced that Homes England’s Strategy and Innovation team has commissioned the Homes that Don’t Cost the Earth (HDCE) partnership of Arup, IIPP, Rising Tide, and Dm. The work runs from October to the end of February 2027 and looks at how more homes can come from the stock we already have. It covers national modelling, delivery prototypes designed with Homes England, and roundtables with investors and local authorities. Max traced the commission back to a Challenge Works mission-shaping process and a Homes England roundtable.

fundraising is like dating, the first 3 dates are only about getting the next one

— Max, passing on a fundraising specialist’s advice

The kick-off took place on 29 October. HDCE’s work is also drawing invitations. Max has been asked to lecture at the Edinburgh Architectural Association in mid-November, and the GLA’s Land and Property team has asked HDCE to present to its development team (thread).

Project manager: Max  ·  Team: Meggan, Oliver  ·  Thanks: Jorn  ·  Partners: Arup, IIPP, Rising Tide
Change orchestration

Cycle two, five weeks from the end

Cycle two of change orchestration runs to the end of November. On 26 October Eunsoo posted a synthesis of the 14 October learning session with experiment leads (full notes in Notion). Capacity is the real limit, and the session asked plainly whether running 17 experiments at once is too many. The hardest problems sit between experiments, so the next step is to test them in clusters, starting with operational flexibility. Health Keepers, the first Dm Signals, Clean Language practice, and time logging are working. Calendar reminders for time logging are not, and Steward momentum has faded since the pop-up.

AI is raising both capability and risk.

— Eunsoo, in the October synthesis

A check before below-minimum bids

Health Keepers agreed a new protocol. Some work reports on costs (Horizon Europe, for example) or pays below the £626 a day minimum. Before you express interest in it, post a short note and tag the Mission & Pathways Stewards. A Steward replies within 72 hours with support, support with conditions, or a veto with a reason. Unless there is a veto, the decision stays with you. Annette, Jorn, Adam, and Leon are the first Stewards testing it, and it will be reviewed in January 2027. It came out of the GGC survey, where 47% chose ‘portfolio hedging’.

Not sure if it applies? Post anyway - helps make uncertainties visible.

— Eunsoo, for Health Keepers

Dm Signals

What Signals turned up

Dm Signals now runs every fortnight. Eunsoo opened rounds on 16 October and 30 October, and Health Keepers reviewed them on 19 October and 2 November. The 6–19 October round had nine inputs and tested AI-generated digests alongside human responses. The 22 October – 2 November round had eight responses, seven of them from Stewards.

The latest round found five themes. There are two speeds, with energy in xCO and heaviness in Dm. Crisis has become the operating rhythm. People asked who decides who gets resourced, and they named role gaps and colleagues going silent. There were positive signals too, about agency and standing. Health Keepers are considering a session on the Dm and xCO relationship at the 10x100 on 18 November.

Both rounds drew mostly on Stewards. If you have not filled in the form yet, it takes a couple of minutes, and every question is optional.

Signals and synthesis: Eunsoo  ·  Review: Health Keepers

The Collaborative: OAK round confirmed

The OAK funding round is confirmed: €5m in total, with €2.325m to distribute in November. After a rushed call for ideas, Dm submitted two: pooling Collaborative input into place-based bioregional demonstrators, and pop-up studios for scoping domains together. Next come two weeks of scoping, two of proposal writing, and an in-person retreat to allocate, which Annette will attend for Dm.

August and September results
